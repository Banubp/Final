Still Curious?

Quick answers to common questions.

What’s your typical design process?

I start with user research and business goal alignment, then move through wireframes, high‑fidelity prototypes, and usability testing. I collaborate with developers and PMs throughout, and I always measure outcomes post‑launch.

Do you work with existing design systems?

Yes. I’m experienced in working inside established systems in Figma and Framer, extending them without breaking consistency. I can also build a new component library from scratch if needed.

What tools do you use daily?

Figma for design and prototyping, Framer for high‑fidelity website builds, and Notion or Linear for project tracking. I adapt quickly to any stack your team already uses.

Can you design and build in Framer?

Absolutely. I offer both UI/UX design and Framer development as separate or combined services. I can hand off designs to your team or deliver a fully built, responsive site myself.

How do you measure the success of a design?

I tie every project to specific business metrics — conversion rates, retention, time‑to‑value, or revenue impact. I define success criteria early with stakeholders and track results after launch.

What does a typical project timeline look like?

A focused sprint (e.g., 5 screens) takes about 2–3 weeks. Larger end‑to‑end engagements run monthly with weekly iterations. I adjust the pace to match your team’s velocity and product cadence.
